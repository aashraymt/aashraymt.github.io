**IDamta LLC, Remote. Jan 2026 to Apr 2026.** Identity and access management.

## What I did

* Configured and tested Okta SSO with OAuth 2.0 and SAML federation across enterprise applications.
* Tested each sign-in workflow for access-control and credential-compromise weaknesses under Zero Trust requirements.
* Diagnosed authentication, provisioning, and role-based access defects during integration testing.

## What I took from it

Identity is where a lot of real attacks start, and I had only seen it from the outside. Setting up federation myself made SAML and OAuth flows much easier to reason about when I test them now.
