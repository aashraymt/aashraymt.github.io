**Eminence Ways, Kathmandu, Nepal. Jan 2025 to Apr 2025.** Penetration testing of web and Android applications.

## What I did

* Found 20+ vulnerabilities (3 Critical, 7 High) in web and Android applications, including IDOR, Stored XSS, and Broken Access Control. Each one went into the report with a CVSS score, a proof of concept, and remediation guidance.
* Built a Python and Bash recon pipeline around subfinder, ffuf, Nmap, and Masscan. It ran across 500+ hosts and handled discovery and result filtering. That left more of my time for manual testing.
* Worked with developers to integrate SAST and DAST tooling into a DevSecOps validation workflow.

Client names and finding details stay with the client.

## What I took from it

This was my first time testing applications that real people used. The findings that mattered most were access-control bugs that no scanner flagged, which is why I automated recon and spent the saved time testing by hand.
