## Cyber@UC CTF team

I have been on the University of Cincinnati's Cyber@UC CTF team since August 2024. I mostly take cryptography and web exploitation challenges. Events so far include MetaCTF and CyberBay CTF.

## National Cyber League, Fall 2025

Our team finished in the top 7% of the Fall 2025 team game. Certificate: [Cyber Skyline scouting report](https://cyberskyline.com/report/C372CP3E068N).

## Teaching

I co-delivered a Burp Suite workshop for 40 Cyber@UC members. We covered HTTP request smuggling, web cache poisoning, and other OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities.

## picoCTF

My first public writeups were three short picoCTF walkthroughs on [Medium](https://aashraymt.medium.com/) in 2025. They are beginner level, and I have left them up as they were.
